Why it was built

Less time learning software.
More time with your photos.

Getting a first image from a telescope like a Seestar is exciting. Figuring out what to do with the files can be harder. EasyAstroEnhance was built to make that next step simple: start with a preset, see the difference, and only reach for more controls when you want them.

01 / Enhance

Reveal faint detail

Reduce uneven sky glow and noise, bring out faint structure, and fine-tune contrast and color. Start with a JPEG, TIFF, or a stacked FITS image.

02 / Stack

Combine exposures

Stack individual FITS exposures, Seestar mosaics, or Moon and planet videos, or combine stacks from several nights. Already have a telescope stack? Import from Seestar adds the newest one in one click.

03 / Compare

Keep your originals

Compare before and after, then save a new image. Your originals stay untouched, and the app doesn't upload your photos or invent new stars.

Your first image

From download to before & after.

  1. Install and open

    Run the downloaded Windows installer, then open EasyAstroEnhance from the Start menu. It opens in your browser, but the app runs on your PC.

  2. Add a photo and enhance

    Start with a telescope JPEG or a stack you already have — Seestar owners can use Import from Seestar…. Keep the default Auto preset, which picks a style for your image, and click Enhance selected. Leave the AI switches off for your first run.

  3. Compare and save

    Check the original against the enhanced image, then choose Download PNG. Don't like a change? Undo, or remove one step from the result. For the steps used and their timings, open More formats → Processing report (JSON). Click Help in the top bar any time for a short guide.

New in 1.7.1. Take a step back: Undo / Redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y), Remove a step from a result, Reset to defaults, go back to an earlier result, or delete one. AI sharpening now sharpens only real detail, so zoomed-in nebulae and galaxies stay smooth instead of blotchy. Edit export… finds the sky position by itself for object labels and lets you pick the font, colour, position and size of your text. A new Help button explains each part of the app.

New in 1.7. Auto picks the look for each image, including a new Moon & planets style. Live preview shows slider changes on the image before you save. Import from Seestar… lists your targets and stacks mosaics and planet videos. Accurate star colours balances colour from the Gaia star catalog, and Edit export… can label galaxies and nebulae and add a compass and scale bar.

Optional AI tools. Background extraction, noise reduction, star separation and the new AI sharpening are all off until you switch them on. With star separation on a linear stack, Star brightness and Star size dim or shrink the stars, and the starless and stars-only layers can be downloaded. Your original and processed-linear FITS keep their stars.

Using a PC without a GPU? Standard enhancement works on the CPU. Optional AI tools need separate model downloads and can take much longer on the CPU, especially noise reduction on larger images.

Good to know

Before you install

Is this a web app or a Windows app?

This site introduces the app and links to the installer. The installed Windows app shows its controls in your default browser, but processes photos on your PC. Standard enhancement works offline after installation; AI models and the optional star and object catalogs are one-time downloads when you choose them. To quit, close the EasyAstroEnhance console window, not just the browser tab.

Why might Windows show a warning?

The current installer is an unsigned preview. Windows SmartScreen may show a warning. Use the download from this page or the official release page rather than an unofficial mirror.

How do I update or uninstall?

Quit the app and find EasyAstroEnhance in Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps. Choose Uninstall. To update, run the new installer under the same Windows account. Uninstalling keeps your history, presets, exports, and downloaded AI models and catalogs.
